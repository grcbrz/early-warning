Monitoring: input drift

Population Stability Index per feature. Warn at 0.10, alert at 0.25. The model's own inputs are the loss-history features; the NDVI alerts trace to the January 2023 switch from the S-NPP to the NOAA-20 satellite, which is why NDVI features are recalibrated per satellite era.

Limits of this analysis

  • NDVI saturates over dense canopy and the monthly AVHRR/VIIRS composites are cloud-affected, so AR1 here is a weak signal. Vegetation optical depth (as in Boulton et al. 2022) is the upgrade path.
  • No tipping threshold has been published for the Congo; nothing on this page estimates one.
  • Hansen "loss" includes natural disturbance and shifting cultivation, and its detection improved after 2011, which inflates the later loss trend.
  • Cells are about 28 km wide. Risk ranks landscapes, not individual plots.
  • Fire data (NASA FIRMS) was not reachable from the build environment and is not used.
